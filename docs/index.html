Section 0.0

Research Abstract

The core findings summarized in five canonical sentences.

Abstract: Identifying high-value search content suffering from organic traffic decay is critical for enterprise SEO efficiency, yet traditional heuristics often trigger false alarms on healthy pages. We evaluated an anonymized corpus of 30,000 enterprise URLs spanning 44 behavioral, engagement, and crawl signals to formulate an automated prioritization system. Using a group-aware, time-honest evaluation split to prevent data leakage, we trained Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM) and Random Forest classifiers against a transparent multi-criteria baseline rule. The learned model achieved a top-50 precision of 0.740 (compared to the baseline's 0.240), representing a ~3× precision lift in correctly detecting traffic-declining assets while uncovering an inverse relationship between unoptimized word count and rank stability. These predictions are operationalized into an actionable four-tier Content Action Playbook that enforces human editorial sign-offs on high-impact interventions.

Section 1.0

Problem Framing & Operational Stakes

Why static heuristic rules fail and how ML transforms content refresh workflows.

The Operational Decision

Content marketing and SEO teams face constrained editorial budgets. Producing new content from scratch costs 5×–10× more than refreshing existing decaying URLs that already possess acquired domain authority.

  • Unit of Analysis: One unique web page snapshot (URL grain) observed over a 90-day trajectory.
  • Stakeholder Action: Editorial copywriters and SEO managers execute queue rewrites based on ranked decay probability.
  • Primary Objective: Maximize Precision@50 to avoid squandering editorial hours on false alarms.

Asymmetric Cost of Errors

Decision errors in search queue allocation carry distinct operational consequences:

False Positive (High Cost): Flagging a healthy or seasonal page as declining causes wasted copywriting spend and risks breaking ranking pages with unnecessary structural changes.
False Negative (Opportunity Cost): Missing an active decaying page leads to direct organic traffic loss and competitor displacement.

Data Safety & Zero-Leakage Contract

Strict governance protocols isolating features from target contamination.

Field Categorization & Exclusion Rationale

Category Sample Fields Operational Purpose & Boundary
Features word_count, cpc, competition, impressions_90d, scroll_rate Measured strictly prior to or at the observation timestamp. Standard scaled & median imputed.
Target / Label is_declining_label Binary classification target derived from 90-day downward trajectory (trend_direction == 'down').
Context Only client_id_hash, snapshot_date Used exclusively for grouped validation splitting (GroupShuffleSplit). Never passed as model inputs.
Excluded trend_direction, trend_pct, Raw URLs, PII Forbidden: Direct target-derived leakage features and private domain information.

Section 3.0

Methodology & Honest Validation Design

Comparing heuristic baseline algorithms against non-linear ML under group-aware partitioning.

1. Baseline Heuristic Rule

To benchmark machine learning fairly, we constructed an intuitive composite heuristic score simulating standard industry practice: 

Action_Score = (Traffic_Decay_Score * 0.60) + ((Search_Volume / 50000) * 0.40)

Reason Codes:
- HIGH_TRAFFIC_DECAY: Severe recent click reduction
- STAGNANT_ENGAGEMENT: High impressions with flat time-on-page
- OPTIMIZATION_WINDOW: High-volume keyword opportunity

2. Machine Learning Architecture

Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM) and Random Forest were deployed with cross-entropy loss and hyperparameter constraints to mitigate overfitting: 

Split Design: GroupShuffleSplit (20% client holdout)
Preprocessing: Median Imputation + One-Hot Encoding
Optimization: Max Depth = 6, Learning Rate = 0.05, n_estimators = 100
Evaluation Metric: Precision@K (K=50) & Group-Aware Macro F1

💡 Key Discovery #1: Search Volume Decoupling

Estimated keyword search volume exhibits virtually zero linear correlation (r = 0.001) with actual 90-day impressions. Optimizing solely for keyword search volume leads to misallocated resources.

💡 Key Discovery #2: Content Length Fallacy

Declining pages had a higher median word count (2,909 words) than growing pages (2,848 words). Simply adding word count without updating query intent alignment does not reverse ranking decay.

Section 5.0

Limitations & Honest Research Claims

Establishing rigorous scientific boundaries on what this system does and does not claim.

✅ Legitimate Claims We Support

  • Observed Statistical Patterns: Measured historical correlations across 30,000 anonymized URLs.
  • Directional Decision Support: Provides ranked prioritization queues to enhance human editorial efficiency.
  • Replicable Signal Lift: ~3× precision improvement over naive hand-rules under strict out-of-client splits.

❌ Strict Negative Scope Boundaries

  • No Causal Claims: Correlation does not imply algorithmic causation without randomized controlled A/B testing.
  • No Google Algorithm Reverse-Engineering: Models internal behavioral telemetry, not search engine weights.
  • No Guarantee of Recovery: Refreshing content does not guarantee ranking recapture if competitors improve faster.
Section 6.0

Operational Content Action Playbook

How ML outputs translate into real-world editorial interventions.

Tier 1: Immediate Structural Rewrite
High Decay Probability (Score > 0.85) + High Historical Traffic Baseline
ACT_01

Comprehensive content restructure, updated headings (H2/H3), refreshed statistics, and search intent alignment. Requires senior SEO sign-off.

Tier 2: Targeted Metadata Refresh
Moderate Decay (Score 0.65 - 0.85) + Stable Impression Volume
ACT_02

Title tag optimization, updated meta descriptions, internal link expansion, and intro paragraph refresh.

Tier 3: Archive & 301 Canonical Consolidation
Low Traffic Baseline + Continuous Decay (> 180 Days)
ACT_03

301 redirect consolidation into relevant parent guides or permanent archive from active index. Requires human approval.

Tier 4: Passive Monitoring Queue
Stable or Growing Trajectory (Score < 0.50)
ACT_04

No immediate operational intervention required. Logged in automated weekly telemetry monitoring.

🚫 The Editorial "No-Go List" (Safety Safeguards)

  • No Autonomous Page Deletion: Removing or unpublishing live URLs without editorial sign-off is strictly prohibited.
  • No Automated Brand Copy Changes: Core brand positioning and legal compliance copy must always pass human review.
  • Confidence Boundary Gate: When prediction confidence falls between 0.50 and 0.75, manual inspection is mandatory.
